# Framework Matrix

Purpose: help curators pick appropriate verbs when linking content nodes to each other and to Framework lenses.
Matrix (Framework → Description → Recommended verbs → Often associated frameworks)

| Framework | Description | Recommended verbs | Often associated frameworks |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAUSE_AND_EFFECT | Lens that foregrounds causal chains, technological or institutional drivers, and immediate impacts; used when the historian is asserting a mechanism or causal pathway. | CAUSES, ENABLES, TRIGGERS, TRANSFORMS | CULTURAL_DIFFUSION, ADAPTATION, CONFLICT_AND_RESOLUTION |
| CULTURAL_DIFFUSION | Focuses on transmission, borrowing, and the spread of practices or ideas across regions and groups; emphasizes routes and agents of transmission. | DIFFUSES, INTRODUCES, ADAPTS, ADOPTS | CAUSE_AND_EFFECT, TEXTUAL_TRANSMISSION, ADAPTATION |
| DOCTRINE_DEVELOPMENT | Emphasizes processes of formalization, systematization, and canonical consolidation within religious or intellectual traditions. | CANONIZES, SYSTEMATIZES, STANDARDIZES, INTERPRETS | TEXTUAL_TRANSMISSION, LEGAL_INTERPRETATION |
| TEXTUAL_TRANSMISSION | Tracks the copying, translation, editorial history, and preservation of texts and textual families. | TRANSMITS, TRANSLATES, PRESERVES, EDITS | DOCTRINE_DEVELOPMENT, CULTURAL_DIFFUSION |
| LEGAL_INTERPRETATION | Applies to jurisprudential, canonical, or administrative rulings and their implementation or reform. | INTERPRETS, CODIFIES, REFORMS, REJECTS | DOCTRINE_DEVELOPMENT, RITUAL_STANDARDIZATION |
| RITUAL_STANDARDIZATION | Focuses on the formalization and institutional adoption of ritual practice across communities. | STANDARDIZES, INSTITUTES, REGULATES | LEGAL_INTERPRETATION, DOCTRINE_DEVELOPMENT |
| GEOPOLITICAL_LINKAGE | Centers on imperial, diplomatic, and territorial relationships that reorganize institutions, mobility, or authority. | LINKS, CONNECTS, RECONFIGURES | CAUSE_AND_EFFECT, CONFLICT_AND_RESOLUTION |
| CONFLICT_AND_RESOLUTION | Captures schisms, wars, negotiations, reconciliations and the social/political effects of conflict. | CAUSES, RESOLVES, RADICALIZES, RECONCILES_WITH | GEOPOLITICAL_LINKAGE, ADAPTATION |
| ADAPTATION | Emphasizes contextual reinterpretation, transformation, and local reworking of imported practices or ideas. | ADAPTS, TRANSFORMS, REINTERPRETS | CULTURAL_DIFFUSION, CAUSE_AND_EFFECT |
| TEMPORAL_LINKAGE | Used for ordering and periodization claims that connect events, reforms, or transitions across time. | PRECEDES, FOLLOWS, IS_ANTECEDENT_TO | CAUSE_AND_EFFECT, CONFLICT_AND_RESOLUTION |

Usage note

- When applying a Framework lens, prefer verbs from the mapped row. If none fit, propose a new verb via the [relations_vocabulary.md](./relations_vocabulary.md) proposal process.

End of framework matrix (draft).
