## The Five Asias

Asia is not one continent — it is at minimum five:

### 1. East Asia — The Economic Titans
**China, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Mongolia, North Korea**

- 1.7B people, ~25% of global GDP
- China: 2nd-largest economy, 1.4B people, Belt & Road reshaping 140+ countries
- Japan: 3rd-largest economy, most aged society on Earth (29% over 65 by 2030)
- South Korea: GDP per capita grew 370x since 1960 ($158 → $32,255)
- Taiwan: Produces 90% of the world's advanced semiconductors (TSMC)
- Mongolia: 1.8 people per km² — least densely populated sovereign nation
- North Korea: Only country to have tested nuclear weapons in the 21st century

### 2. South Asia — The Population Engine
**India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bhutan, Maldives, Afghanistan**

- 2.0B people (25% of humanity)
- India: Surpassed China as most populous (1.44B), world's 5th-largest economy
- Pakistan: 240M, nuclear-armed, 7th-most-populous country
- Bangladesh: $2,688/capita but garment industry supplies 84% of global fast fashion
- Bhutan: Only carbon-negative country on Earth, measures Gross National Happiness
- Maldives: Highest country is 5.1m above sea level — existential climate threat
- Sri Lanka: 2022 economic collapse, debt trap case study

### 3. Southeast Asia — The Diversity Belt
**Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam, Thailand, Myanmar, Malaysia, Cambodia, Laos, Singapore, Timor-Leste, Brunei**

- 700M people, ASEAN collective = world's 5th-largest economy
- Indonesia: 17,508 islands, 270M people, largest Muslim-majority nation
- Vietnam: GDP grew 8% annually for 30 years — fastest sustained growth in region
- Singapore: 734 km² → 4th-highest GDP per capita on Earth
- Philippines: 7,641 islands, 115M people, 10M overseas workers (10% of population)
- Cambodia: Angkor Wat complex = largest religious structure ever built
- Thailand: Only Southeast Asian country never colonized

### 4. Central Asia — The Heartland
**Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan**

- 75M people, post-Soviet since 1991
- Kazakhstan: 9th-largest country, more land than Western Europe combined
- Uzbekistan: Double-landlocked neighbor Liechtenstein aside, no coastline within 2 borders
- Turkmenistan: 4th-largest gas reserves, most closed society after North Korea
- All 5 countries gained independence on the same year (1991) with no independence movements

### 5. West Asia (Middle East) — The Energy Nexus
**Saudi Arabia, Iran, Iraq, Israel, UAE, Turkey, Syria, Jordan, Lebanon, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Qatar, Yemen**

- 400M people, ~55% of global oil reserves
- Saudi Arabia: Vision 2030 attempting post-oil transformation, $500B NEOM megaproject
- Iran: 88M people, ancient civilization, international sanctions since 1979
- Israel: 9.8M people, 42nd in global innovation, startup nation
- Turkey: 85M, straddles Europe/Asia, NATO member with Ottoman legacy
- Iraq: Cradle of civilization (Mesopotamia), 20 years post-invasion reconstruction
- UAE: Dubai went from fishing village to global hub in 50 years
- Syria: 13-year civil war, 6.8M refugees (largest displacement since WWII)

## Hidden Patterns

### 1. The Asian Return, Not Rise
Asia produced 60-70% of global GDP until ~1800. The "rise of Asia" is actually a **restoration** to historical baseline after 200 years of European colonial disruption. China and India alone accounted for 50% of global output for most of recorded history.

### 2. The Semiconductor Chokepoint
Taiwan (TSMC) produces 90% of the world's advanced chips. South Korea (Samsung) produces most of the rest. A single earthquake, invasion, or drought in Taiwan could collapse the global economy. No other supply chain bottleneck in history has been this concentrated.

### 3. The Youth-Age Divide
Japan (median age 49), South Korea (44), China (39) are aging rapidly. India (28), Philippines (26), Afghanistan (18) are young. By 2050, half of Asia's workers will be in South Asia — but the wealth is in East Asia. This is a continental collision in slow motion.

### 4. The Water Wars Are Already Here
China's Three Gorges and Mekong dams control water for 60M+ people downstream (Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos, Thailand). India and Pakistan share the Indus. Turkey's GAP project controls the Euphrates for Syria and Iraq. Every major Asian river is now a geopolitical weapon.

### 5. The Colonial Clock Resets Differently
Unlike Africa (arbitrary borders, CFA franc), Asia's colonial legacy manifests as:
- **Partition violence:** India/Pakistan (1947), Korea (1953), Vietnam (1954)
- **Unresolved territories:** Kashmir, Palestine, Taiwan, Tibet, Kurds
- **Military base networks:** US bases in Japan, South Korea, Philippines, Bahrain
- **Language fracture:** Unlike Africa's French/English dominance, Asian languages survived colonialism

### 6. Religion as Geopolitical Force
No other continent has religion so tightly woven into modern politics:
- Islam shapes governance in 15+ nations (Saudi, Iran, Pakistan, Afghanistan, Indonesia)
- Buddhism drives identity in Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Cambodia
- Hinduism defines India's political trajectory (BJP/RSS)
- Judaism anchors Israeli statehood
- Confucianism structures East Asian governance philosophy
- Christianity shapes Philippines and South Korean culture

### 7. The AI Triad
Three countries will determine Asia's (and the world's) AI future: China (state-directed, surveillance-integrated), Japan (robotics + aging society), and India (talent pipeline, 4.4M developers). Everything else is commentary.
